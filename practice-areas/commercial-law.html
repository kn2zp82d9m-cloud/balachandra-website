Commercial Law

Commercial disputes, corporate matters and business transactions before the Commercial High Court of Colombo.

Overview

Practical support for commercial matters

Commercial disputes — from unpaid trade debts to shareholder disagreements and contractual breakdowns — are typically heard before the Commercial High Court of Colombo, which was established to deal with commercial matters above a prescribed monetary threshold with a degree of procedural efficiency not always available in general civil litigation.

I advise businesses and individuals on commercial contracts, corporate structuring questions, and dispute resolution, drawing on my ongoing LLM specialisation in Commercial Law at the University of Colombo.

What This Involves

Understanding Commercial Law in Sri Lanka

✓
Commercial contracts — supply, service, and trade agreements need to be clearly drafted to be enforceable.
✓
Corporate matters — company formation, governance, and compliance are governed by the Companies Act.
✓
Commercial disputes — disputes over trade debts, breach of contract, and business torts are heard before the Commercial High Court.
✓
Shareholder and partnership disputes — disagreements between business co-owners can be resolved through negotiation or litigation.
✓
Business debt recovery — outstanding commercial debts can be recovered through the courts.
✓
Arbitration — the Arbitration Act provides an alternative to litigation for resolving commercial disputes.
How It Works

The Process

01
Consultation
Understanding the commercial relationship, the dispute or transaction at hand, and the desired commercial outcome.
02
Document & Contract Review
Careful review of agreements, correspondence, and financial records relevant to the matter.
03
Negotiation or Filing
Where possible, resolution is pursued through negotiation; otherwise proceedings are filed before the Commercial High Court or an arbitral tribunal is engaged.

Settlement, award, or judgment, followed by appropriate enforcement steps where required.
Legal Framework

Relevant Law

  • Companies Act No. 7 of 2007 — governs incorporation, governance, and regulation of companies.
  • Commercial High Court of Colombo (Establishment) Act — establishes the jurisdiction of the Commercial High Court.
  • Arbitration Act No. 11 of 1995 — governs domestic and international commercial arbitration.
  • Sale of Goods Ordinance — governs contracts for the sale of goods.
Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What qualifies as a commercial matter for the Commercial High Court?
Specified categories of commercial disputes above a prescribed monetary threshold, including matters relating to companies, banking, insurance, and commercial contracts.
Is arbitration available for commercial disputes?
Yes, under the Arbitration Act No. 11 of 1995, and many commercial contracts include arbitration clauses as an alternative to litigation.
What is the minimum claim value for the Commercial High Court?
The Commercial High Court generally hears disputes exceeding a specified monetary threshold; matters below that value are typically heard in the District Court.
Can foreign companies bring claims in Sri Lankan courts?
Yes, subject to procedural rules and, in some instances, requirements as to security for costs.
