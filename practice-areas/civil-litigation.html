Overview

Resolving civil disputes efficiently

Civil actions in Sri Lanka — including debt recovery, breach of contract, and claims for damages — are generally heard before the District Court under the Civil Procedure Code. These matters often involve pre-trial demand, pleadings, discovery, and, if unresolved, a full trial on the merits.

I act for both plaintiffs seeking recovery or redress and defendants resisting claims, with an emphasis on early resolution where possible and firm representation at trial where necessary.

What This Involves

Understanding Civil Litigation in Sri Lanka

✓
Debt recovery — unpaid dues, loans, and outstanding invoices can be recovered through the District Court.
✓
Breach of contract — claims may arise from a failure to perform contractual obligations.
✓
Damages — claims in delict/tort address loss caused by wrongful acts or negligence.
✓
Injunctions — interim relief may be sought to preserve property or rights pending trial.
✓
Recovery of possession — actions may be brought to recover possession of movable or immovable property.
✓
Partition and co-ownership — disputes between co-owners of property can be resolved through partition proceedings.
How It Works

The Process

01
Consultation & Merits Assessment
Review of the facts, documents, and evidence to assess the strength of the claim or defence and the likely forum.
02
Pre-Action Demand
Where appropriate, a formal letter of demand is sent, which can resolve a dispute before litigation becomes necessary.
03
Filing the Plaint
If unresolved, the action is filed before the District Court, with the defendant served and given an opportunity to answer.
04
Trial & Evidence
Presentation of evidence and legal argument before the District Court through to judgment.
05
Judgment & Execution
Where judgment is obtained, appropriate steps are taken to execute the decree and recover what is due.
Legal Framework

Relevant Law

  • Civil Procedure Code of Sri Lanka — governs the procedure for civil actions before the District Court.
  • Prescription Ordinance — sets time limits within which civil actions must be brought.
  • Debt Recovery (Special Provisions) Act No. 2 of 1990 — applicable to certain institutional debt recovery actions.
  • Law of Contract (general law) — governs the formation and breach of contractual obligations.
Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How long do civil cases take in Sri Lanka?
Duration depends on complexity, the court's workload, and whether the claim is contested. Undefended actions typically conclude faster than fully contested trials.
What is the limitation period for a contract claim?
Under the Prescription Ordinance, most contract-based claims are subject to a prescriptive period, commonly six years for actions on a written contract, though this depends on the specific facts.
Can I get an injunction before trial?
Yes, an interim injunction may be sought under the Civil Procedure Code to preserve the subject matter or prevent irreparable harm pending trial.
What if the other party doesn't respond to the claim?
Where the defendant fails to appear or answer after proper service, the plaintiff may apply for judgment in default.
